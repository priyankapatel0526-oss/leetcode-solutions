# Two Sum

## Problem: Two Sum (Easy)

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/two-sum/

### Approach

I used two loops to check every possible pair of numbers.
If the sum of two numbers is equal to the target, their indices are printed.

### Complexity

- Time: O(n²)
- Space: O(1)

### Notes

I tested the program with two test cases in VS Code.
The first test case checks a normal example, and the second checks duplicate values.
